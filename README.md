# Frugal & Free Printables

Free budget printables from [Frugal & Free](https://www.pinterest.com/frugalandfree_) — simple, one-page tools to help you spend less and save more.

## What's here

- **monthly-budget-tracker.pdf** — a free one-page monthly budget tracker. Print it, fill in your income and spending, and see where every dollar goes. Read the guide: [Free Monthly Budget Tracker Printable](https://algoritmaai.blogspot.com/2026/11/free-monthly-budget-tracker-printable.html)
- **pin-03-monthly-budget-tracker.png** — the Pinterest pin image for the tracker.

## Savings challenges

- **52-Week Savings Challenge** — save $1 in week 1, $2 in week 2, all the way to $52 in week 52. Math, not magic: $1 + $2 + … + $52 = $1,378 in a year. The pin is on our Pinterest.
